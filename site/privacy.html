RugbyAnalysis

Privacy policy

For the RugbyAnalysis app and rugbyanalysis.com. Last updated 9 November 2026.

The RugbyAnalysis app shows international and club rugby fixtures, results, tables and predictions, can send you alerts about the games you care about, and lets you chat with friends you invite. It keeps as little about you as it can: no email address, no location, and no real name unless you choose to use it in chat. This page explains what it keeps and why.

Who we are

RugbyAnalysis is run by Brendon Fourie, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@rugbyanalysis.com.

What the app keeps

  • An anonymous ID. The app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication, which gives it a random ID. There is no account, email address or password.
  • The teams and clubs you follow, and any teams you've muted.
  • Your alert settings: which alerts you want (weekend heads-up, big games, kick-off reminders, full-time scores) and any match you asked to be reminded about.
  • Your phone's time zone offset (for example, one hour ahead of UTC), so alerts give kick-off times in your time.
  • A notification token, only if you turn alerts on in the Android app. Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone this token, and we use it only to send you the alerts you chose.

The app does not ask for or use your location, contacts, photos or anything else on your phone. It does not show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.

On the website: links we share carry a tag such as ?ref=ig-bio. The website counts visits by that tag and the page opened, so we know which posts send people to it. It sets no cookies and records nothing about you or your device.

Who can see it

Your teams, clubs and alert settings: nobody else. The database only lets your own copy of the app read or change your record, and our server code reads it only to work out which alerts to send you. Chat names and messages are seen by the people in the same room, and our server code reads new messages only to send alerts about them to the room's other members. We don't sell or share your information with anyone except the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase runs the anonymous sign-in, the database (including chat), the server code that sends alerts, and the notifications themselves. The database and server code are in Google's London region (europe-west2). See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts rugbyanalysis.com, where the app downloads fixtures, results and predictions. Like any website, it receives the information every web request carries, such as your IP address.
  • Links you choose to open, such as our Instagram or Facebook page, open in that app or your browser, and its own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

Your record is kept while you use the app, and deleted automatically once the app hasn't been opened for a year. You can delete it sooner at any time in the app: Me > Your data > Delete my data. That removes it from our server straight away, turns alerts off, takes you out of your chat rooms, deletes your messages and deletes the rooms you made. Chat messages are deleted 30 days after they're sent in any case. If a phone's notification token stops working (for example, after you uninstall the app), the server deletes that token the next time it tries to send an alert. See how to delete your data for other ways.

Our legal basis

We use this information to give you the service you ask for in the app: your teams, the alerts you turned on, and chat (UK GDPR article 6(1)(b)). We keep reports so we can keep chat safe, which is in our and our users' legitimate interests (article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. The quickest way to delete it is in the app. For anything else, email hello@rugbyanalysis.com and we'll reply within a month. If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

If you're in South Africa

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) applies to the information described here. The information officer is Brendon Fourie, at hello@rugbyanalysis.com. Your information is stored in Google's London region, outside South Africa, with the same protections described on this page. We use it because it's needed for the service you asked for (POPIA section 11(1)(b)). If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

The app isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change how the app uses your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
