Delete your data

For the RugbyAnalysis app, by Brendon Fourie. Last updated 6 November 2026.

The RugbyAnalysis app has no accounts with names or email addresses. It keeps a small record under an anonymous ID: the teams you follow, your alert settings and, if you turned alerts on, your phone's notification token. Here's how to delete it.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open RugbyAnalysis and go to Me.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete my data, then Delete.

Your record is deleted from our server straight away, along with the anonymous ID, and alerts stop.

By email

If you can't use the app, email hello@rugbyanalysis.com with the subject Delete my data. Because we don't hold your name, email address or phone number, we can only find your record from details the app holds, so the in-app option is the surest way. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The teams you follow and mute, your alert settings and your match reminders.
  • Your phone's notification token and time zone offset.
  • The anonymous sign-in ID.

What is kept

Nothing. We don't keep copies after deletion. See the privacy policy for more.
