The 2026 Spain Sevens or SVNS VLL was a rugby sevens tournament played at Estadio José Zorrilla in Valladolid, Spain. Twelve men's teams and twelve women's teams participated.[1]

It is the second leg of the HSBC SVNS World Championship.
