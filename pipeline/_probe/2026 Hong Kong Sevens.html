The 2026 Hong Kong Sevens or SVNS HKG was a rugby sevens tournament played at Kai Tak Stadium. Fifteen men's teams and fifteen women's teams participated.

It is also the first leg of the World Championship Series.[1] In addition to the SVNS tournament, there was also an invitational competition called the Melrose Claymores.
