The 2026 World Rugby Pacific Nations Cup[a] was the eighteenth Pacific Nations Cup (PNC) tournament. The tournament featured a condensed version, hosted over two matchdays in Japan between four teams with semi-finals, a third-place game and a final.[2]

Fiji were the defending champions, going into the tournament having won the previous two editions back-to-back (2024, 2025).[3] Japan won the tournament for the fourth time after defeating Fiji 20–15 in the Grand Final.[4] Canada defeated the United States 19–16 for third place.[5]

Background

[edit]

The 2026 edition of the PNC tournament featured a new four-team format with Japan, Fiji, Canada, and the United States.[2] Semi-finals were held in Ōsaka on 12 September, followed by the third-place play-off and final in Tokyo on 19 September.[2] Defending champions Fiji were aiming for a third consecutive title.[2]

The competition formed an important part of preparations for the 2027 Rugby World Cup in Australia and aligns with World Rugby's revamped international calendar.[2] Although Samoa and Tonga did not compete in this edition, they instead played in the new Nations Cup in July and continue to receive development support.[2][6]
