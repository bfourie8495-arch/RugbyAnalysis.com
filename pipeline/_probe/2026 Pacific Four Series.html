The 2026 Pacific Four Series was the sixth edition of the Pacific Four Series, an international women's rugby union tournament, that took place from 11 to 25 April 2026.[1][2]

The series started in the United States with all four teams competing in Sacramento and Kansas City.[1] The final round saw the United States and Canada play their match in Chicago,[1] followed by Australia hosting New Zealand on 25 April 2026 on the Sunshine Coast in a historic, first-ever test match between both nations played on Anzac Day.[3]

New Zealand retained their Pacific Four Series title after a 40–5 victory over Australia on the final match day of the Series.
