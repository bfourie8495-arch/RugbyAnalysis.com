The 2026 Rugby Europe Women's Championship is the 29th edition of Rugby Europe's first division competition for women's national rugby union teams. It began on 28 March and conclude on 18 April 2026.[1]

Belgium entered as the new team in the Championship, they were promoted after Sweden requested to return to the Trophy division due to internal circumstances.[2]
