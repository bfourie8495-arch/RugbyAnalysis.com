The 2026 France Sevens or SVNS BDX is a rugby sevens tournament scheduled to be played at Stade Atlantique in Bordeaux, France. Twelve men's teams and twelve women's teams will participate.[1]

It is the third and final leg of the HSBC SVNS World Championship.
